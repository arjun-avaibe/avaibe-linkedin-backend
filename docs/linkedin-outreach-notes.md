# LinkedIn Business Outreach: Evaluation Notes

*Working notes for the AVAIBE LinkedIn project. Last updated 2026-11-11.*

**Scope:** evaluate *every* LinkedIn activity that can grow relevant UAE business interest, not only posting.
**Primary goal:** reach and convert **UAE decision-makers, especially schools** (principals, owners, directors, school IT).
Follower count alone is not success.

---

## 1. The funnel everything is measured against

| Stage | What it means | Key measures |
|---|---|---|
| **Awareness** | Relevant people see AVAIBE | Est. UAE impressions, UAE / education / decision-maker share of viewers |
| **Interest** | They look closer | Page visits, founder profile views, new page followers (UAE share), newsletter subscribers |
| **Consideration** | They engage with intent | Website clicks, event registrations, meaningful comments from the target group, inbound messages |
| **Conversion** | Business conversation | Qualified enquiries, demos/meetings booked, proposals |
| **Outcome** | Revenue and proof | Deals won, case studies, referrals, recommendations |

Every activity below should move at least one stage *for the target segments*. Activity that only grows vanity
metrics (likes from irrelevant audiences) is a cost, not a win.

## 3. How to evaluate any activity

For every activity, record:

1. **Hypothesis:** e.g. *"Founder comments on UAE principals' posts will raise education-sector page follows."*
2. **Target segment:** A (UAE decision-makers), B (tech), C (partners), D (general).
3. **Effort and cost:** hours/week and money.
4. **Primary metric** (one) and **guardrail metric** (e.g. the UAE share mustn't fall).
5. **Baseline:** the same metric for the 4 weeks before.
6. **Test period:** usually 4–6 weeks, long enough to beat noise.
7. **Attribution:** how a result is linked to the activity (see section 4).
8. **Decision rule:** keep / adjust / stop. For example, stop if the primary metric doesn't beat the baseline after 6 weeks.

Then compare activities by **relevant result per hour** and **per dirham**, not by raw likes.

## 4. Attribution (linking outreach to business results)

- **UTM links** on every LinkedIn link to the website, so website analytics show which post or activity sent the visitor.
- **"How did you hear about us?"** on the enquiry form, with LinkedIn options (post, founder, event, ad, message).
- **Source field in the CRM** for every lead; ask sales to fill it in.
- **LinkedIn Lead Gen Forms and event registrations** are attributed automatically.
- **Enquiry log in our app** (planned): date, source activity, segment, qualified yes/no, outcome.
- Accept that some influence is indirect (e.g. someone saw five posts, then searched Google). Look at trends over
  time, not single-touch credit.

## 5. Data access and limits

| Data | Source | Automatic? |
|---|---|---|
| Company page posts and stats | Community Management API (pending) | Yes, once approved |
| Page follower/visitor demographics | API (pending) or manual weekly | Yes once approved |
| Who saw each post (UAE / education share) | Manual from post analytics | No |
| Founder post stats | Manual (API access is restricted) | No |
| Comments made, invites sent, messages, events held | Manual activity log | No (and shouldn't be automated) |
| Website visits from LinkedIn | Website analytics with UTM links | Yes, if UTMs are used |
| Enquiries and deals | Enquiry form / CRM | Depends on CRM |
| Competitors | Public pages, manual review | No |

**Privacy:** store only business-relevant, publicly available information about people. Don't scrape LinkedIn or
store private personal data. Check prospect tracking against the UAE personal data protection law (Federal
Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021) with whoever handles legal/compliance.

## 6. What this means for the app (proposed)

1. **Activities module:** a weekly action plan (suggested by the Assistant) and a quick **activity log** (comments made,
   invites sent and accepted, reshares, events, messages, ads running).
2. **Enquiry log with source attribution:** every enquiry tied to a channel and activity.
3. **UTM link builder:** generates tracked links for posts, newsletters and events.
4. **Funnel dashboard:** awareness → interest → consideration → conversion, per segment and per asset
   (company page, Abhilash, Anoop).
5. **Activity impact view:** each activity's metric against its baseline, with the keep/adjust/stop decision.
6. **Assistant scope:** suggestions cover *all* activities above, not only posts. For example, which UAE school leaders'
   posts to engage with this week, which connections to invite to the page, and event and newsletter topics.
7. **Warm-prospect list:** repeat engagers from the target segments (public, business-relevant info only).
8. **Competitor tracker:** public activity of 10–15 UAE competitors.
